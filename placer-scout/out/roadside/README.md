# Roadside check: light chute seen from I-15 (Nov 1, 2026)

- Viewpoint: I-15 southbound, San Bernardino County postmile R122.5 = 35.1187, -116.2348 (Caltrans SHN_Postmiles_Tenth service).
- Matched by a simulated view (`panorama.py`): USGS 3DEP 1/3" DEM tile n36w117 + Sentinel-2 L2A S2B_11SNU/11SNV 2026-10-19 colors (`s2_mosaic.py`). Scripts were run from a scratch folder; paths inside them are relative.
- Chute: ravine on the east face of the southwest Soda Mountains, about 35.1196, -116.3020, ~6 km west of the viewpoint, draining east onto East Cronese Lake.
- Land: BLM; inside the Soda Mountains Wilderness (designated 2019, BLM NLCS layer) and the Soda Mountains WSA ACEC (BLM ACEC layer).
- Nearest MRDS gold records (USGS MRDS WFS): unnamed Au-Cu-Fe past producer and Au-Cu prospect ~5.4 km west, on the far side of the range (35.111, -116.360/-116.364); Smith (Au) ~9 km south. None drains into this chute.
- Reading: the pale fill looks like wind-blown sand packed into the ravine (a sand ramp), matching pale sand in other ravines on the same face. This is an interpretation from 10 m imagery, not a field check.
